# MatchPrism — Persona Exploration

**Question:** When someone hears "MatchPrism" and visits matchprism.com, who are they and what do they hope to find?

---

## Category A: Career & Professional Matching

### 1. Priya — Job Seeker (26, India)
**Hears "MatchPrism" and thinks:** "Finally, something that tells me which jobs actually fit me, not just keyword matching."
**Excited because:** She's tired of applying to 200 jobs on LinkedIn. Wants to see her skills scored against a role like a compatibility percentage. "You're a 87% match for this Product Manager role — here's why, and here's what's missing."
**Would pay:** $15/month if it actually reduced her applications from 200 to 20 targeted ones.

### 2. Raj — Hiring Manager (38, UK)
**Hears "MatchPrism" and thinks:** "A tool that shows me candidate-job fit at a glance."
**Excited because:** He reviews 300 resumes per role. Wants a prism that breaks down a candidate into dimensions (technical, cultural, experience, growth potential) and scores them against his requirements.
**Would pay:** $99/month per seat. Would push to his company's recruiting budget.

### 3. Meera — Freelancer (31, remote)
**Hears "MatchPrism" and thinks:** "Match me with the right clients, not just any gig."
**Excited because:** She wastes hours on Upwork bidding on wrong projects. Wants to see project-freelancer fit scoring. "This project needs React + fintech + US timezone — you're 92% fit."
**Would pay:** $20/month if it replaced her Upwork premium.

### 4. Tom — Career Changer (34, US)
**Hears "MatchPrism" and thinks:** "I'm in marketing but want to move to product. Show me where I already match and what gaps I need to fill."
**Excited because:** Career transition advice is generic. He wants data: "Your skills match 68% of PM roles. The gap: SQL (take this course), A/B testing (you already have this from marketing)."
**Would pay:** $29 one-time for a career transition report.
