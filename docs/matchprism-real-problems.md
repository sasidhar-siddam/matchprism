# What People Are Actually Struggling With (2026)

Data from Reddit (100K+ upvotes), search trends, and consumer research.

---

## The Top Pain Points (ranked by upvotes + urgency)

### 1. "I'm paying for stuff I forgot about" (150K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** Subscription creep. People paying $100-500/month for forgotten services.
**Why it persists:** Apps like Truebill/Rocket Money exist but don't handle digital estate (what happens when you die?), and many miss subscriptions buried in PayPal, Stripe, App Store.
**AI-proof?** Partially — needs bank integration + persistent tracking. AI can't see your bank account.
**MatchPrism fit:** LOW — not a "matching" problem.

### 2. "This contractor is ripping me off" (80K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** No way to verify contractor pricing, quality, or reliability before hiring.
**Why it persists:** Angie's List/HomeAdvisor are lead-gen for contractors, not protection for homeowners. Fair pricing data doesn't exist publicly.
**AI-proof?** YES — needs local pricing databases, verified review aggregation, real-time availability.
**MatchPrism fit:** HIGH — "match me with a trustworthy contractor for my specific job." This IS a matching problem.

### 3. "I can't tell if these reviews are fake" (30K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** Amazon, Google, Yelp reviews are gamed. Consumers can't trust them.
**Why it persists:** Fakespot/ReviewMeta exist but are browser extensions nobody installs. Platforms profit from fake reviews.
**AI-proof?** PARTIALLY — AI can analyze reviews, but needs persistent databases of known fake patterns, seller histories, review velocity tracking.
**MatchPrism fit:** MEDIUM — could be "match me with products that have REAL reviews" but it's a feature, not a product.

### 4. "Everything is getting worse on purpose" (enshittification)
**Problem:** Products degrade over time (more ads, higher prices, removed features). Consumers have no way to track this.
**Why it persists:** Nobody systematically tracks product quality over time. Norwegian Consumer Council published a report on it. Cory Doctorow wrote a whole book.
**AI-proof?** YES — needs historical tracking. AI can't tell you "Notion added 3 paywalled features in the last 6 months" without historical data.
**MatchPrism fit:** HIGH — "show me how this product has changed over time" = a prism revealing the true trajectory. SignalArk's historical data is EXACTLY this moat.

### 5. "I'm lonely and can't make adult friends" (60K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** Adults can't find genuine connections matching their specific interests, values, life stage.
**Why it persists:** Meetup is events, not relationships. Facebook Groups are broadcasting, not connecting. Bumble BFF is dating-app mechanics for friendship (doesn't work).
**AI-proof?** YES — needs a two-sided marketplace with real profiles, mutual matching, ongoing relationship tracking.
**MatchPrism fit:** VERY HIGH — "match me with people I'll actually click with" is THE core promise of the name. But dating/friendship apps are brutally hard to bootstrap (chicken-and-egg).

### 6. "Meetings are killing my productivity" (89K upvotes)
**Problem:** Organizations waste massive time/money on unnecessary meetings.
**AI-proof?** NO — Calendly, Clockwise, AI meeting summarizers already exist and work.
**MatchPrism fit:** LOW.

### 7. "Nobody taught me how to adult" (40K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** No comprehensive resource for practical life skills (taxes, negotiation, home buying, legal rights).
**AI-proof?** NO — ChatGPT is already a better "adulting guide" than any app could be.
**MatchPrism fit:** LOW.

### 8. "I'm doom-scrolling and can't stop" (70K+ upvotes)
**Problem:** Algorithmic manipulation of attention. Users want to reclaim their information diet.
**AI-proof?** PARTIALLY — needs device-level integration (screen time APIs, browser extensions).
**MatchPrism fit:** LOW — not a matching problem.

### 9. "Product search is broken" (47% frustration rate)
**Problem:** Google results are ads + SEO spam. People add "reddit" to every search because they trust real opinions over marketing.
**Why it persists:** Reddit itself is now testing AI shopping search (Feb 2026) because they know people come to Reddit FOR purchase decisions.
**AI-proof?** TRICKY — AI search is getting better, BUT Reddit just launched product carousels. The window may be closing.
**MatchPrism fit:** MEDIUM — was our original thesis but Reddit itself is solving this.
